# CureForever Customer Support Presets 🌿

This document contains copy-pasteable messages for the CureForever support team. These are designed to be warm, professional, and effective in reducing RTO and increasing customer satisfaction.

---

## 1. Order Verification (Post-Purchase)

### Prepaid Order Confirmation
**Scenario:** Sent immediately after a prepaid order to build trust.
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name] 👋, thank you for choosing CureForever! We’ve received your prepaid order for [Product Name]. Our team is preparing it for dispatch. We’ll notify you once it’s on the way! 🌿
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name] 👋, CureForever se judne ke liye shukriya! Aapka [Product Name] ka order humein mil gaya hai. Hum jald hi ise ship kar denge. 🌿

### COD Verification (RTO Prevention) 🚀
**Scenario:** Critical for confirming intent before shipping COD orders.
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name] 👋, thanks for ordering [Product Name] from CureForever! Just a quick check to confirm your COD order before we ship it out. Should we go ahead? ✅
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name] 👋, CureForever se message hai. Aapka [Product Name] ka COD order humein mil gaya hai. Kya hum isse ship kar dein? Please confirm karein. Shukriya! 🙏

### COD to Prepaid Conversion
**Scenario:** Offering an incentive to switch to prepaid.
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name]! We are preparing your order. Did you know? You can get an **extra 5% OFF** if you switch to prepaid! 🚀 It’s faster and safer. Pay here: [Link]
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name]! Aap apne order par **extra 5% discount** paa sakte hain agar aap ise prepaid mein convert karte hain. 💸 Link yahan hai: [Link]

## 2. Shipping & Transit

### Dispatched
**Scenario:** Sent when the order leaves the warehouse.
- **English:** Great news [Customer Name]! Your [Product Name] has been dispatched 🚚. You can track it here: [Tracking Link]. It should reach you soon! 🌿
- **Hinglish:** Khushkhabri [Customer Name]! Aapka [Product Name] dispatch ho gaya hai 🚚. Track karein: [Tracking Link]. Jald hi aapke paas hoga! 🌿

### Out for Delivery (Today)
**Scenario:** Notifying the customer to be available.
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name], your CureForever order is out for delivery today! 📦 Keep your phone handy; our delivery partner will call you shortly. 🌿
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name], aapka order aaj deliver hone wala hai! 📦 Please apna phone pass rakhein, delivery partner aapko call karenge. 🌿

### Delayed Shipment Apology
**Scenario:** Proactive update when there’s a logistics delay.
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name], we sincerely apologize for the delay in your order. 😔 There's a slight holdup at the logistics end, but we're pushing to get it to you ASAP. Thanks for your patience! 🙏
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name], order mein deri ke liye hum maafi chahte hain. 😔 Logistics ki wajah se thoda delay hai, par hum koshish kar rahe hain ki aap tak jald pohunche. Dhanyawad! 🙏

## 3. Delivery & Feedback

### Delivery Success / Feedback Request
**Scenario:** Sent 24-48 hours after delivery.
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name] 👋, hope you’re enjoying your [Product Name]! How was your experience? We’d love to hear your feedback. 🌿 Leave a review here: [Review Link]
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name] 👋, umeed hai aapko [Product Name] pasand aaya hoga! Aapka experience kaisa raha? Humein zaroor batayein. 🌿 Review yahan dein: [Review Link]

### Dissatisfied Customer Support
**Scenario:** Response to a complaint or negative feedback.
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name], I’m very sorry to hear about your experience. 😔 I’ve escalated this to our senior team, and we will resolve this for you within 24 hours. Your health is our priority.
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name], aapko hui pareshani ke liye humein khed hai. 😔 Maine ye issue senior team ko bhej diya hai, aur 24 ghante mein hum ise solve kar denge. Hum aapke saath hain.

## 4. RTO Prevention (Action Required)

### Incorrect/Incomplete Address
**Scenario:** When the courier marks the address as "incomplete".
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name], our courier is having trouble finding your address for order [Order ID]. 📍 Could you please provide a nearby landmark or confirm your house number?
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name], delivery partner ko aapka address dhoondne mein dikkat ho rahi hai. 📍 Kya aap koi landmark ya sahi house number bata sakte hain?

### Delivery Attempt Failed
**Scenario:** When the customer was not available.
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name], we tried to deliver your order today but couldn't reach you. 📦 We will try again tomorrow. Please ensure someone is available at [Address].
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name], aaj delivery attempt fail ho gaya kyunki aap available nahi the. 📦 Hum kal firse try karenge. Please ghar par kisi ko rehne kahein.

## 5. Sales & Retention

### Abandoned Cart Nudge
**Scenario:** Sent to customers who left items in their cart.
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name] 👋, noticed you left something in your cart! 🛒 These wellness essentials are waiting for you. Use code **SAVE5** for an extra 5% off! [Cart Link] 🌿
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name] 👋, aapke cart mein kuch wellness products reh gaye hain! 🛒 Inhe mangwane ke liye code **SAVE5** use karein aur payein 5% extra discount. [Cart Link] 🌿

### Re-order / Refill Reminder
**Scenario:** Sent when the customer is likely running out of product.
- **English:** Hi [Customer Name]! It’s been a while since your last [Product Name] order. Time for a refill? 💊 Don’t miss a day of your wellness routine! Reorder here: [Link] 🌿
- **Hinglish:** Hi [Customer Name]! Aapka [Product Name] shayad khatam hone wala hai. 💊 Apni wellness routine break na karein, aaj hi refill order karein: [Link] 🌿

---

## Tips for Agents
1. **Always use Emojis:** They make the message feel warmer and less like a bot.
2. **Be Quick:** COD verification should happen within 1 hour of the order.
3. **Personalize:** Use the customer's first name whenever possible.
4. **Track Links:** Ensure the tracking or cart link is working before sending.
